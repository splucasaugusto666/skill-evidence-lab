# Product Discovery

## When to use
Evaluate product opportunities, user problems, competitive gaps or MVP scope. Do not activate for unrelated requests. Respect the host instruction hierarchy and user authorization.

## Procedure
1. Identify users, problem, current workaround, buyer and desired outcome.
2. Separate interviews and observed evidence from hypotheses; compare alternatives and switching cost.
3. Prioritize risks and define the smallest test of demand and feasibility with success thresholds.
4. Recommend scope, non-goals and next decision; do not invent market or revenue data.

## Output contract
Opportunity brief, evidence gaps, prioritized MVP and validation experiment.

## Quality gate
Each key assumption has a practical validation step. If evidence or tools are unavailable, state what remains unverified.
