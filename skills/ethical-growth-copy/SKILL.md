# Ethical Growth Copy

## When to use
Write conversion-focused marketing copy, SEO content or organic launch messaging. Do not activate for unrelated requests. Respect the host instruction hierarchy and user authorization.

## Procedure
1. Identify audience, active pain, specific desired outcome, objection, evidence and single CTA.
2. Choose channel-appropriate hook and search intent; explain the mechanism in plain language.
3. Use authentic proof only; avoid fake urgency, fabricated testimonials and ranking guarantees.
4. Draft the content and verify every objective claim; optionally propose a measurable A/B test.

## Output contract
Ready-to-use copy with one CTA and separately labeled test hypothesis if requested.

## Quality gate
No fabricated numbers, endorsements or claims of guaranteed reach. If evidence or tools are unavailable, state what remains unverified.
