# Fitness Programming

## When to use
Plan evidence-informed general exercise sessions or review a training program. Do not activate for unrelated requests. Respect the host instruction hierarchy and user authorization.

## Procedure
1. Collect goals, training experience, availability, equipment and relevant restrictions; do not diagnose medical conditions.
2. Select movement patterns, progression and recovery appropriate to the stated level; explain the rationale.
3. Specify sets, repetitions, rest and effort targets, with alternatives and adjustment rules.
4. Flag pain or concerning symptoms for qualified clinical review; distinguish general education from individualized medical advice.

## Output contract
Practical schedule, exercise prescriptions, progression and safety notes.

## Quality gate
No invented clinical diagnosis, guaranteed results or unsafe progression. If evidence or tools are unavailable, state what remains unverified.
